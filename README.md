On supported Apple devices, the app may use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework to request permission before accessing tracking-related information or allowing tracking as defined by Apple.

The system prompt may ask whether you want to allow the app to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

You may select:

- **Ask App Not to Track**

Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from using the app. Advertisements may still be displayed, but they may be less personalized, contextual, non-personalized, limited, or based on information that does not require tracking permission.

You can review or change tracking permissions through:

## 23. Definitions

- **Advertising Data:** Information concerning advertisements displayed, viewed, or interacted with.
- **AI Interactions:** Prompts, outputs, uploaded files, attachments, and associated metadata.
- **Controller / Data Fiduciary:** The organization that determines why and how personal data is processed.
- **Device Identifier:** An identifier associated with a device, including an advertising identifier where available.
- **Personal Data:** Information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, as defined under applicable law.
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, analysis, and deletion.
- **Processor / Service Provider:** An organization that processes information on behalf of another organization.
- **Tracking:** Linking data collected from an app with third-party data for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing data with a data broker, as defined by Apple.
- **User Content:** Prompts, images, files, audio, messages, and other information supplied by a user.
